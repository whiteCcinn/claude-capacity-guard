# Code of conduct

Participants should make collaboration safe and useful for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements on their merits, give actionable feedback, and accept corrections in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information, and persistent disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's credentials, private transcripts or contact details without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or close discussions when necessary to enforce these expectations. Responses should consider context and impact. Maintainers are expected to apply the same standards to themselves.

For a conduct concern, request a private contact channel through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/whiteCcinn) or a public issue without identifying private details. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports should be handled discreetly; no absolute confidentiality guarantee is implied by a public issue.
